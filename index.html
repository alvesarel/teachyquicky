How the videos are made

Every video is original. It is scripted, narrated and animated in-house with the help of AI tools, then checked and published by the TeachyQuicky creator. Where a platform offers an AI-generated label, the videos carry it.

TeachyQuicky Uploader

TeachyQuicky Uploader is the desktop tool that publishes TeachyQuicky's finished videos. It runs on the creator's own computer and has no sign-up.

  • YouTube: uploads and schedules the videos on the TeachyQuicky channel.
  • Instagram and Facebook: publishes them as Reels on the TeachyQuicky account and Page.
  • TikTok: after the creator signs in with TikTok, it sends each finished video to the TeachyQuicky TikTok inbox as a draft. The creator reviews it, adds the caption and the AI-generated label, and posts it in the TikTok app.

It accesses only what the Privacy Policy lists, under the Terms of Service.
