TeachyQuicky Uploader: Terms of Service

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. What the tool is

TeachyQuicky Uploader is a desktop tool that publishes TeachyQuicky's own finished videos to TeachyQuicky's accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. On TikTok it only sends the video to the account's inbox as a draft. The creator reviews and posts it in the TikTok app.

2. Who uses it

The TeachyQuicky creator uses it for TeachyQuicky's own accounts. It has no sign-up and isn't offered for other accounts. Connecting an account requires that account owner's consent on the platform's own sign-in page, and the owner can revoke it at any time (see the Privacy Policy).

3. Content

The tool publishes only original TeachyQuicky videos, made in-house with the help of AI tools and labelled as AI-generated where the platform offers it. It does not copy content from other creators or platforms, and it adds no watermark or promotional text when sending a video to TikTok.

4. Platform rules

Every upload is subject to the destination platform's terms and rules, including the YouTube Terms of Service, the Meta Terms and Instagram Terms of Use, and the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. The creator is responsible for each post.
