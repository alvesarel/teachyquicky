TeachyQuicky Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

TeachyQuicky Uploader is the publishing tool of TeachyQuicky, an educational channel of short "how stuff works" videos. It runs on the TeachyQuicky creator's own computer and publishes TeachyQuicky's own videos to the creator's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. It has no other users, no sign-up and no server of its own.

What it accesses

Only with the account owner's consent, given on each platform's own sign-in page:

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): upload videos (youtube.upload) and read the channel's basic details (youtube.readonly) to confirm uploads go to the right channel.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta Graph API): publish Reels to the TeachyQuicky Instagram account and Facebook Page, and read their IDs.
  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API): read the account's open ID and display name (user.info.basic) to confirm it is the TeachyQuicky account, upload videos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload), and read their upload status.

What it stores

The access and refresh tokens for those accounts and the IDs above, saved only in files on the creator's own computer, plus a local record of which video was sent where. It has no analytics, cookies or ads, and collects no data about anyone else.

Who receives data

Only the platform a video is published to receives it, with its title, caption and tags: Google/YouTube, Meta or TikTok. Each handles that data under its own policy: the Google Privacy Policy, the Meta Privacy Policy and the TikTok Privacy Policy. Using the YouTube upload means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Meta: Facebook → Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Profile → Menu → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions → TeachyQuicky Uploader → Remove access.

Revoking access makes the stored token stop working. The tokens are kept until access is revoked or the creator deletes the token files, which can be done at any time.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and collects no data from them.
